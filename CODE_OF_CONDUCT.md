# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, ability, ethnicity, identity,
experience, nationality, appearance, religion, or background. We pledge to act
in ways that keep the community open and welcoming.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback with grace.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other people.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **contact@hopenmind.com**. Reports
are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions and comments that do not align with this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge harmful.
